Dimensionless parameters that measure rigidity of a planetary body/gravitating object and susceptibility of its shape to change in response to external tidal potential. There are 3 love numbers

- **h** is defined as ratio between body tide to the height of the static equilibrium tide
- **k** is defined as the cubical dilation or the ratio of the additional potential produced by the deformation of the deforming potential
- **I** represents the ratio of the horizontal displacement of an element of mass of the planet's curst to that of the corresponding static ocean tide
