Dictates the predictability of the system. The evolution of it from numerical integration shows a marked difference between regular and chaotic behavior. Regular tend to have a slope of -1 in $\log\Lambda$ versus $\log t$ while chaotic orbits tend to have some positive value of $\Lambda$.
Defined as $\Lambda=\lim_{ t \to \infty } \dfrac{1}{t} \dfrac{\ln(|\delta \mathbf{Z(t)}|)}{\delta \mathbf{Z_{0}}}$; where $\Lambda$ is the Lyapunov exponent and $\delta \mathbf{Z_{0}}$ is an infinitesimal vectorial separation in phase space
