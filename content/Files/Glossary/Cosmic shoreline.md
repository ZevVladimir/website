A potential statistical trend that provides a way to divide exoplanets into what planets that do and do not have an atmosphere. Compare how much light a planet receives from its star and how readily the planet's atmosphere escapes into space (proxy for gravity). A delineation in planetary escape velocity versus lifetime incident XUV radiation space between planets with and without atmospheres [[bradyEarthlikeDensityTemperate2025]].
