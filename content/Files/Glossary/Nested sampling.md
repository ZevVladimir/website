Sampling from the likelihood function $\mathcal{L}$ within a bound $\mathcal{L}>k$ while increasing $k$ iteratively upon reaching convergence to evaluate the posterior is better than directly sampling $\mathcal{L}$.

Has compared to direct sampling
- Lower computational complexity
- Ability to explore multi-modal distributions of arbitrary complex shapes
- Ability to numerically evaluate the Bayesian evidence $Z$
