Terms that depend on the planet's mean motion, n, and other higher-order orbit terms are ignored. Describes the system's [[Secular Evolution]]. Most 2 planet systems predicts that the eccentricities oscillate. Also predicts the behavior of the difference in the two [[Longitude of Pericenter]]s $\Delta \tilde{\omega}$ depending on initial conditions. Aligned libration oscillates around 0. Anti-aligned libration around $2\pi$ or circulates through $2\pi$.
