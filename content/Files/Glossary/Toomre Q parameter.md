Reveals stability within a portion of a differentially rotation disk. A value of $Q<1$ indicates instability with $Q>1$ being **Toomre's stability criteria**. The instability comes from the tendency of higher-density locations on the disk to gain density. Analagous to the Jeans criterion with the inclusion of the disk's rotation and the effects of self gravity of the disk material.

For molecular clouds $Q>1$ indicates no star formation while $Q<1$ indicates there is.

Assumes that the disk is made of a homogenous substance. If it is not sometimes a higher Q threshold can be assumed ($>3$)

[[hopkinsFORGEdFIREResolving2024]] defines it as $Q_{x}\approx\sigma_{x}\kappa/\pi G\Sigma_{\mathrm{disk}}$
- $\sigma_{x}=$
	-  $c_{s}$ for the thermal $Q_{\mathrm{thermal}}$ 
	- $v_{A}$ for magnetic $Q_{\mathrm{mag}}$
	- $\delta v_{\mathrm{turb}}$ for turbulent $Q_{\mathrm{turb}}$
- $\sigma_{\mathrm{eff}}^{2}=c_{s}^{2}+v_{A}^{2}+\delta v^{2}_{\mathrm{turb}}$ for total effective $Q_\mathrm{eff}$
