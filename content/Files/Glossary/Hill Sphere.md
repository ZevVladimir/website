Model of gravitational sphere of influence within which one body 
dominates over the influence of other bodies. The radius is the Hill Radius $R_{H}=r(\dfrac{M_{p}}{3M_{\star}})^{1/3}$ . The parameter $p^2E$ where E is the energy and $p$ is the angular momentum of the system determines regions of allowed motions. Binary system is *Hill stable* against disruption if $p^2|E|\geq$ the critical value derived for corresponding 3 body Hill surface.
