RV signal as a function of time resulting from the orbit of a planet

Given by $f(t;K,P,e,\omega,M_{0})=K[\cos(\omega+\nu(t;e,P,M_{0}))+e\cos\omega]$
Where $K,P,e,\omega,M_{0}$ are the [[Radial velocity signal amplitude]], orbital period, orbital eccentricity, the [[Argument of periastron]], [[Mean Anomaly]]. The [[True anomaly]] is $\nu$
