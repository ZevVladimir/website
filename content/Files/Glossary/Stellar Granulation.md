Three types: granulation, meso-granulation, and super-granulation. These are a convective phenomenon that affect RV measurements. Can be found all over the surface except in active regions where convection is significantly lower. Amplitudes are similar to ones from pressure modes [[Stellar Oscillation]]. [[dumusquePlanetaryDetectionLimits2011]]

Granulation: lifetime < 25 min, diameter < 2 Mm
Meso-granulation: between granulation and super-granulation in lifetime and size
Super-granulation: lifetime up to 33 hr on the sun, diameter 15-40 Mm
