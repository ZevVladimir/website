Pressure waves or p-modes propagate from the surface of stellar type stars resulting in dilation and contraction of external envelopes over a few minutes. RV signature varies between $10-400 cms^{-1}$ . Amplitude and period of oscillation modes increases with mass along the main sequence. [[dumusquePlanetaryDetectionLimits2011]]
