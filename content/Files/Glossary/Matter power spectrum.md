tags: [cosmology, statistics]
aliases: [power spectrum, P(k)]
---
- Captures the variance of matter density fluctuations as a function of scale
- Quantifies two-point correlation structure of the density field in Fourier space
	- $\langle \delta(\boldsymbol{k})\delta \boldsymbol{k}' \rangle =(2\pi)^{3}\delta^{3}_{D}(\boldsymbol{k+k'})P(k)$
		- $\delta_{D}$ is the Dirac delta function
	- $P(k)$ provides insights into amplitude and scale-dependence of matter clustering
