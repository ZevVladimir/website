Take the velocity power spectrum based off either a FFT algorithm or a weighted periodogram and multiply the found power by the effective length of the observing run (the inverse of the area under the spectral window) giving the spectral energy distribution in that unit time. [[dumusquePlanetaryDetectionLimits2011]].
Going from RVs to VPSD and back does not lose any information.
