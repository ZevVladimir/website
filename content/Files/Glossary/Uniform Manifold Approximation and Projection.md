Based on three assumptions:
1. Data is uniformly distributed on [[Riemannian manifold]]
2. The [[Riemannian metric]] is locally constant
3. The manifold is locally connected

Can then model the manifold with a [[Fuzzy topological space]]. Then find an embedding by finding a low dimensional projection of the data that is has the closest possible equivalent fuzzy topological structure
