The relation between the orbital inclination and the semimajor axis, according to the following expression: $b=\dfrac{\cos i\times a}{R_{s}}$ with inclination $i$, semi major axis $a$, stellar radius $R_{s}$. 
It is defined by the smallest apparent distance between the two centers of the bodies in units of $R_{s}$. A high impact parameter implies a grazing transit which produces a gradual ingress and egress. The impact factor and the semi-major axis can be used to infer the inclination relative to the observer
