Even if a star is moving at a constant space velocity it can appear to have a change in radial velocity. 
	1. taking the derivative of $v_r=-v\cos \phi=\dot{v_r}=\frac{dv_r(t)}{dt}=v\sin \phi \cdot \dot{\phi}=v_t\dot{\phi}$   
		1. Tangential velocity depends on the distance of the star and its proper motion ($\dot{\phi}$)
		2. These can change and thus can make it appear as if the RV is changing
	2. Only geometrical and not physical and is always positive 
	3. Section 3.1 of https://arxiv.org/pdf/0908.0944
