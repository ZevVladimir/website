M(t) is an angle related to the fictitious mean motion around the orbit used to calculate the [[True anomaly]]. Defining the mean motion as $n \equiv \dfrac{2\pi}{P}$ then $M(t)=\dfrac{2\pi}{P}(t-t_{p})=n(t-t_{p})$. It is also related to the [[Eccentric anomaly]] given by Kepler's equation: $M(t)=E(t)-e\sin(E(t))$
