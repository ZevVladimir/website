Defined with respect to the reference plane with range $0 \leq i < 180 \degree$ with $i=0\degree$ a face-on orbit. Prograde is in direction of increasing angle on the sky if $i<90\degree$ and retrograde if $i>90\degree$ and projected onto the line of nodes if $i=90\degree$
