## Conclusion
- Present framework for modeling formation of galaxies of luminosities up to $\sim L_{\star}$
- Show model can simultaneously match observational constraints on the stellar mass - halo mass relation and observed relations between stellar mass and gas phase and stellar metallicities, gas mass, size, star formation rate, etc.
- Show models with values for wind parameters close to those motived by energy driven wind model and FIRE simulations can reproduce observational constraints
- The shape of the $M_{\star}-M_{h}$ relation and thus the $L_{V}-M_{h}$ relation on dwarf scales is controlled by
	- Mass dependence of the mass loading factor of outflows $\eta_{W}$
	- Gas accretion suppression in small mass halos after reionization
- Suppression of gas accretion from UV heating after reionization results in steepening of $M_{\star}-M_{h}$ relation for $M_{\star}\approx10^{4}-10^{6}M_{\odot}$ and flattening at $M_{\star}\lesssim 10^{4}M_{\odot}$
- Halo occupation by galaxies with $M_{V}<0$ is consistent with observational constraints in the model with $z_{\mathrm{rei}}\lesssim9$
	- Late reionization of the LG volume is favored
- Fiducial model reproduces observed relations
	- $M_{\star}-M_{h}$
	- $M_{\star}-Z_{\star}$
	- $M_{\star}-Z_{g}$
	- $M_{\star}-r_{1/2}$ relation of dwarf galaxies in the LG
	- Surface brightness distribution of observed dwarfs
- Fiducial model matches data for $M_{\star}-M_{g}$ relation
- Model galaxies have SFRs that match those of low surface brightness dwarfs but underestimate it in blue compact dwarfs
	- Interpreted as a signature of strong burstiness of star formation in dwarf galaxies
- Galaxies in fiducial model have diverse star formation histories
- Results of the model indicate that several properties of dwarf galaxies and their distributions are signatures of reionization

> We present a simple regulator-type framework designed specifically for modelling formation of dwarf galaxies. Despite its simplicity, when coupled with realistic mass accretion histories of haloes from simulations and reasonable choices for model parameter values, the framework can reproduce a remarkably broad range of observed properties of dwarf galaxies over seven orders of magnitude in stellar mass. In particular, we show that the model can simultaneously match observational constraints on the stellar mass--halo mass relation, as well as observed relations between stellar mass and gas phase and stellar metallicities, gas mass, size, and star formation rate, as well as general form and diversity of star formation histories (SFHs) of observed dwarf galaxies. The model can thus be used to predict photometric properties of dwarf galaxies hosted by dark matter haloes in $N$-body simulations, such as colors, surface brightnesses, and mass-to-light ratios and to forward model observations of dwarf galaxies. We present examples of such modelling and show that colors and surface brightness distributions of model galaxies are in good agreement with observed distributions for dwarfs in recent observational surveys. We also show that in contrast with the common assumption, the absolute magnitude-halo mass relation is generally predicted to have a non-power law form in the dwarf regime, and that the fraction of haloes that host detectable ultrafaint galaxies is sensitive to reionization redshift ($z_{\rm rei}$) and is predicted to be consistent with observations for $z_{\rm rei}\lesssim 9$.
