# Vocab

- **Compressible Flow**: changes in the fluid density are important
- **Incompressible Flow**: flows where density changes in the fluid are not important for the physics
- **Fixed region (FR):** One where the control surface does not move at all and is fixed in space
- **Material Region (MR)**: One where the surface moves with the local velocity of the material
- **Volume Region (VR):** Where the surface velocity is the same at each location but varies with time
- **Arbitrary Region (AR):** A region which does not fit any of the other regions
- **No-slip condition:** Molecules leaving a surface and moving back into either fluid have the same tangential velocity

# Notes

## Continuum Assumption

- The continuum assumption means that the physical properties are imagined to be distributed throughout space
    - Every point in space has some value although there might be discontinuous jumps there should not be infinities or undefined at a single point

## Density, Velocity, and Internal Energy

- Density: $\rho = \lim_{L\to 0} \frac{\sum m_i}{V}$
    - L never reaches a molecular scale but goes to 0
- Fluid Velocity
