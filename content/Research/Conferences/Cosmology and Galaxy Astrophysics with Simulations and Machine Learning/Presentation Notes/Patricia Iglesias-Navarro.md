## Inferring stellar population properties from resolved photometry with simulation-based inference

- Pixel based SED fitting
    - Chi-square minimization has degeneracies
    - Bayesian search is too slow
    - So simulate synthetic data for forward model fit to observations then compare to truth
- Study evolution and gradients of physical properties in galaxies across redshifts
- Can use to look at star formation and structure evolution
