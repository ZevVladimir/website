## The PRIYA Simulation Suite

- For Lyman alpha forest cosmology
    - Traces weakly nonlinear DM
    - Find cosmological params from this
- Only up to z=2
- Relatively insensitive to AGN bcs z=2
- There is a scale-dpendent correlation for helium reionization
- Observed 1D flux power mixes model for the power spectrum
- Use multi-fidelity gaussian process to convert power spectrum to get cosmological parameters
    - Build model with low resolution
    - Correct with high resolution, dependent on cosmology
