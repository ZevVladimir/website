## Bridging Observations and Simulations: UV Luminosity Functions of Star-Forming Galaxies at z~0.5

- Use CAMELS to get galaxies to feed into SYNTHESIZER to forward model and generate SEDs and photometry to measure UVLFs
- Then use UVLFs to train NN with priors to predict astrophysical parameters to then analyze data from satellites
- Can study parameter variations to understand galaxy formation/evolution
