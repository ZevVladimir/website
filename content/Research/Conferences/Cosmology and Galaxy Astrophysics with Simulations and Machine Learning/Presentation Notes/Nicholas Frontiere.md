## The Impact of Exascale Simulations

- Much faster 10-50x with GPU acceleration
- Allows for an order of magnitude of better resolution (trillions for hydro 10s of trillions for gravity only)
- Have full sky particle light cones, full merger trees, full particles
- Can generate synthetic sky catalogs
