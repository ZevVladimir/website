## Probing Baryonic Feedback and Cosmological Tension with Fast Radio Bursts: Insights from CAMELS simulations

- Baryons suppress matter power spectrum
- Use fast radio bursts (FRBs) as an observable for this
    - Look at the dispersion measure: electron column density along line of sight
- Measure the F-parameter using CAMELS
- Strong correlation between S(pread?) and F-parameter
    - FRBs/F-param would be a good observational measure
- But CAMELS too small so looking at larger sims to be able to compare to observables
