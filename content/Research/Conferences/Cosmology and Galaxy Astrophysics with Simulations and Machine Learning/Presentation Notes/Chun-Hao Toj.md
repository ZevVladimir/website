## Simulations for Current and Future Lensing Surveys: From DES to Roman

- Difficult to go from actual observations to mass or galaxy density maps
- Want sims to have accurate small-scale modeling with large volume and many realizations
- Solution is to use paired high and low-res simulations
- This can be used to validate and assess information from DES and ROMAN
