## Direct Estimation of Galaxy Cluster Mass Accretion Rates using Machine Learning

- Galaxy clusters are influenced by mass accretion rate
- Use X-ray and tSZ mock observations to train CNN and then use normalizing flow model to get uncertainties
- Can get better predictions of mass accretion
- Interpretation: if you convert the images into a radial profile then the model uses both this and the asymmetry components
