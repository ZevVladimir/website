## Reconstructing Star Formation Histories Using Neural Density Estimation and Hydrodynamic Simulations

- Modeling galaxies SED
- Size of error bars is dependent on chosen prior
- So use physically motivated model
    - Based on sims with star formation as a product of hydrodynamical sim
    - Also realistic simulated photometry
- Parametrize the simulation in terms of mass versus lookback time
    - Can accurately reproduce SFR versus lookback time
- Then use SBI
- Then compare to real observations (COSMOS)
    - Use **Prior Predictive Checking** to see if model is reasonable
    - Use outlier detection: only 2% of real galaxies rejected. Simulated data good for predicting?
