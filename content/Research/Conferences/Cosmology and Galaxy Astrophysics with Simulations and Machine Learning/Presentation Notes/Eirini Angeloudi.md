## Inferring the 2D ex-situ stellar mass distribution of galaxies with diffusion models

- Affect of galaxy mergers and detecting them using an archaeological approach
    - What mergers are needed to create the galaxy we see today
    - Use SBI
- Use observables to find fraction of mass/stars that are in-situ vs ex-situ
- Then go from 1D fraction to 2D fraction using diffusion models
