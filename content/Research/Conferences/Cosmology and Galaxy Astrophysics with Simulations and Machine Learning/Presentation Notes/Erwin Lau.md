## X-raying CAMELS: Constraining CGM physics with eRASS

- CGM is between stellar and AGN dominated feedback
- eROSITA
    - Used to measure CGM with stacking
- Modern sims do not agree with eRASS
    - Use CAMELS to create emulators for XSB prfls and Lx[luminosity]-Mstar relation
    - Simulations seem too low at large radii for X-ray surface brightness
    - More sensitive to SN than AGN but could be data bias
    - Use eRASS to get constrains on feedback
        - Larger SN1 and AGN1 and lower SN2 and AGN2
        - But these do not match the Mstar-Mhalo relation
    - Could be an impact due to contaminations from X-ray binaries and other effects leading to overestimate of X-ray CGM
