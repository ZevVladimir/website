## Constraints on Cosmology and Astrophysics with Observed Galaxy Clustering: the new photometric expansion to CAMELS-SAM

- CAMELS-SAM: middle ground with moderate volumes/resolution with flexibility and speed
    - Have physically motivated prescriptions for gas, stars, metals using halo merger trees
- LtU Connections: pipeline for inference in observational plane using physics based galaxy formation models
- Working on photometry
    - Have full histories of star formation
    - Then compress this history using **dense basis**
    - Then apply stellar population synthesis model to obtain photometry to get mock observations
