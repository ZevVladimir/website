## Leveraging Generative Models

- Simulation-based inference (SBI) assumes training simulations overlapped with reality which could be biased
- Use generative models (neural likelihood estimator)
- Training data: weak lensing (WL) maps from DM only sims
- Test data: WL maps with baryonic effects
- Want test to be distinguishable from training
- Field level performs better than summary statistics
