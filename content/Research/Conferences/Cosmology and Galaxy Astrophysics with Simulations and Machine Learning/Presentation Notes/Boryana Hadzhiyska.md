## Detecting large baryonic feedback around DESI photometric galaxies

- Use kSZ to find optical depth and halo velocity. Identify strong baryonic feedback from kSZ
- Specifically get stacked kSZ and calculated velocities to find optical depth
- Can be used to inform galaxy-galaxy lensing
- Also find cosmic filaments from DESI data and calculated gravitational potential
    - Find alignment of gas along filaments
    - Different than what sims produce especially with Illustris which has isotropic energy which won’t produce these anisotropies
