## How DREAMS are made: Emulating Satellite Galaxy and Subhalo Populations with Diffusion Models and Point Clouds

- Find a way to go from dark matter nbody to galaxies
- Learn conditional likelihoods between sim → halo → central galaxy → satellites
- Model galaxy clusters as a point cloud
- Can use a diffusion model to do this
