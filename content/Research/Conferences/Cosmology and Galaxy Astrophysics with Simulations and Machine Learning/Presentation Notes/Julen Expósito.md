## A probabilistic deep learning model to infer DM distribution of dwarf galaxies with HI gas and stellar kinematics

- NN trained on sims can infer density profiles skipping uncertainties from dynamical modeling
    - Hydro sims
- Use CNN trained on many projections of each galaxy
- Allow for determining if the galaxies have a core
- Then expand this to generating full density profile
- Then add information about rotation curves
    - When added with everything before does even better
