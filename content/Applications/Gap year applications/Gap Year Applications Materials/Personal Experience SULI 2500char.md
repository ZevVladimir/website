Through a range of professional and extracurricular experiences that have strengthened my communication, leadership, and collaboration skills I will be able to contribute meaningfully to the SULI program beyond the skills I gained from my academic and research work. These experiences have taught me how to navigate complex technical challenges, support others, and how to lead, making me well-prepared for the dynamic, team-oriented environment of a national laboratory.

As the project lead of SATFAB, a student-led project at the University of Maryland chapter of Students for the Exploration and Development of Space (SEDS), I led a multidisciplinary team focused on building modular, custom, and low-cost cube-satellite components. I managed timelines and deadlines and coordinated between sub-teams working on everything from mechanical structures to avionics development. I also handled communication with professors, launch providers, and external aerospace contacts and ensured that we met strict performance, budgetary, and integration requirements. Finally, I developed onboarding materials to streamline the process for our newer members and organized team social events. Balancing leadership responsibilities and requirements with hands-on engineering work taught me how to manage both people and problems, a skill I believe will serve me well in a national lab.

In addition to technical leadership, I’ve worked extensively as a tutor and mentor. I served as a tutor for math, reading and writing for students from 3rd to 7th grade. I worked closely with my students to explain concepts they were struggling with and developed activities to help keep them engaged and interested in learning. I also mentored first-year astronomy students at the University of Maryland, where I meet weekly with students to provide academic guidance and help them transition to and succeed in college life. These experiences have made me a more thoughtful communicator and listener, while teaching me how to break down complicated topics.

Altogether, my experiences leading a cube-satellite team, mentoring peers, and working as a tutor have helped me build both the technical know-how and the communication skills needed to thrive in a collaborative research environment like the SULI program. I’m excited by the chance to work alongside researchers on real scientific challenges, and I’m confident I can bring both initiative and a strong sense of teamwork to the table.
