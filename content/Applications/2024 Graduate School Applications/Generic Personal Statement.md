This preconception has both motivated me to succeed in an area that has been deemed hard, but to also work to counter this notion.

I was fortunate enough to grow up in a school district with a robust AP program, where I had the opportunity to take both AP Physics 1 and AP Physics C. Even more impactful than the courses themselves were the two exceptional teachers who inspired and motivated me. Their passion for teaching made physics not only approachable but enjoyable, showing me and my peers that while physics can be challenging, it is also deeply rewarding. This experience sparked my desire to pursue a degree in physics and astronomy at the University of Maryland (UMD), where I could continue to discover exciting applications of physics while working to inspire others to explore the wonders of astrophysics.
