	- [x] Research
	As an undergraduate I worked with Dr. Benedikt Diemer to publish a first author paper that has been accepted for publication in the Astrophysical Journal. I developed a machine learning model to determine if particles in dark matter only simulations belonged to a dark matter halo dynamically. The model used significantly less data that particle trackers while maintaining very high accuracy that exceeded those of faster but less accurate phase space cut methods. I have also been working with Dr. Jacob Bean at the University of Chicago as a post-baccalaureate research to reduce data from the MAROON-X instrument to determine the radial velocities of stars in order to identify rocky exoplanets around M-dwarfs. Finally, I have also been working with Dr. Andrey Kravtsov at the University of Chicago to run N-body simulations to determine if UMaIII/U1, the smallest Milky Way satellite found, is a dwarf galaxy or a star cluster but identifying if there would be any observable stellar streams.
