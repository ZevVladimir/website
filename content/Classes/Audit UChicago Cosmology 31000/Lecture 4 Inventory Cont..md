# Baryons
- For cosmologists: All things in the standard model
- Quarks
	- u d c s b t
	- Can't exist freely except in super early universe
	- $p \in uud$
	- $n\in udd$
	- $\pi \in u\bar{u}\;\;d\bar{d}$
- Leptons
	- $e^{-}\nu_{e};\;\mu^{-}\nu_{\mu};\;\tau^{-}\nu_{\tau}$
	- $\mu$ and $\tau$ decayed into $e^{-}$
	- Can basically ignore as they contribute $<0.1\%$ mass
- Bosons
	- $\gamma;\;W^{\pm};\;Z;\;g;\;H$
	- $W,\;Z,\;H$ all decayed and $g$ doesn't exist alone
## Measuring $\Omega_{b}h^{2}$
1. Big Bang Nucleosynthesis (BBN)
	- Ratio of Deuterium to Hydrogen $\dfrac{D}{H}$ is very sensitive to $\Omega_{b}h^{2}$
	- Find $\Omega_{b}h^{2}=0.0222\pm0.0005$
2. CMB anisotropies
	- $\Omega_{b}h^{2}=0.0225\pm0.0003$
	- We know $h\approx0.7$ so $\Omega_{b}\approx0.045$
	- Only $4-5\%$ of the energy density is in baryons
3. Dark Matter
	- 3 Methods to measure
		1. CMB anisotropy
			- $\Omega_{m}h^{2}=0.1431\pm0.0025$
			- $\Omega_{m}=0.315\pm0.007$
			- Need DM to explain the discrepancy between $\Omega_{m}$ and $\Omega_{b}$
		2. Measure distances $D_{L}(z)$ and $D_{A}(z)$
			- $\chi=\int \dfrac{dz}{H(z)}=\dfrac{1}{H_{0}} \dfrac{dz}{\sqrt{ \Omega_{m}(1+z)^{3}+\dots }}$
			- From DESI: $\Omega_{m}=0.2975\pm0.0086$
			- Assuming that Dark Energy is a cosmological constant
		3. Measure galaxy clustering and shape distortions
			- DESY3: $\Omega_{m}=0.339\pm0.03$
			- Will be updated 01/22/2026
4. Dark Energy
	- From CMB
		- $\Omega=1$
			- With $\Omega_{m}=0.3$ 
			- Then $\Omega_{DE}=1-\Omega_{m}=0.7$
	- Important piece of evidence
		- Measure supernova distances $D_{L}(z)$ vs $z$
		- Find that $\chi$ is larger than expected
	- Define $\rho_{DE}\propto(1+z)^{(1+w)}$
		- From conservation of $T_{\mu \nu}$
			- $w=\dfrac{P}{\rho}$
			- $T^{\mu}_{\nu}=\mathrm{diag}(-\rho,P,P,P)$
		- Consider version of GR
			- Ricci tensor $R_{\mu \nu}=-\dfrac{1}{2}g_{\mu \nu}R+\Lambda g_{\mu \nu}=8\pi GT_{\mu \nu}$
			- Move $\Lambda g_{\mu \nu}$ to RHS
			- $8\pi G\left[ T_{\mu \nu}-\dfrac{\Lambda}{8\pi G}g_{\mu \nu} \right]$
			- Define $T^{\Lambda}_{\mu \nu}\equiv -\dfrac{\Lambda}{8\pi G}g_{\mu \nu}$
			- Multiply by $g^{\mu'\mu}$
			- $\left( T^{\Lambda} \right)^{0}_{0}=-\rho^{\Lambda}=-\dfrac{\Lambda}{8\pi G}g^{00}g_{00}$
			- $\rho=\dfrac{\Lambda}{8\pi G}\mathrm{const}$
			- $\left( T^{\Lambda} \right)^{1}_{1}=g^{11}\left( -\dfrac{\Lambda}{8\pi G} \right)g_{11}$
			- $P^{\Lambda}=-\dfrac{\Lambda}{8\pi G}=-\rho^{\Lambda}\implies w=-1$
5. Neutrinos
	- Characteristics
		- Fermions
		- Interact weakly
			- $\sigma \sim \dfrac{\alpha^{2}E^{2}}{m_{Z}^{4}}$ for $e^{+}e^{-}\to \nu$
		- 3 generations of $\nu$ with $\nu \bar{\nu}$ but each only has one spin state
		- $0.06\mathrm{eV}\lesssim\sum_{\nu}m_{\nu}\lesssim0.5\mathrm{eV}$
