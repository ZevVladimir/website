# Abstract

- Together with extreme precision radial velocity (EPRV) measurements from ESPRESSO have identified and reinforced evidence for planet candidates b, c, d, e around Barnard’s Star
- Minimum masses range from $0.19-0.34M_\oplus$ current data rules out planets with masses $>0.57M_\oplus$ in the habitable zone
