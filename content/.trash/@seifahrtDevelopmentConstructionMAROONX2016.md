# Abstract

- MAROON-X Is designed to find and characterize rocky planets around nearby M dwarfs
- Based on commercial echelle spectrograph customized for high stability and throughput

# Main Paper

- Radial velocity measurements can deliver confirmation and mass measurement for candidate transiting planets. Also help support direct imaging efforts by identifying and constraining planets
- Scientific mission: detect the reflex motion of very low-mass stars due to the gravitational influence of an Earth-size planet orbiting in its habitable zone
- Optimal wavelength to observe M dwarfs for high precision radial velocities (based on sim results) is in the red part of the optical
- Limiting factor: the distances to the nearest transiting, habitable zone, earth size planets are larger than those to the nearest such non-transiting planets
- Components/features
    - Echelle spectrograph with high throughout and thermo-mechanical stability
    - Fiber feed with low focal-ratio-degradation and high scrambling efficiency
    - Pupil slicer and image scrambler to match the slit-resolution produce to the $A\Omega$ produce of a 6.5m telescope and increase the near and far field stability of the light injection
    - Fiber agitator to suppress modal noise and speckle effects
    - A fabry-perot etalon as a wavelength calibrator capable of delivering <10cms^-1 stability of timescales of minutes to months and a high information density to measure and correct for instrumental drifts
