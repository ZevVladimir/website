# Overview

- 5 sentences
- Can share your stories
- Random starting sentence to start each day
- Different days have different rules for different sentences
    - Different word limits per sentence
    - Have to use a certain word
- Time limit for each sentence about a minute
- Can share with friends or share to public place
    - There can upvote for best story
    - Story at the end of day gets an award for best story of the day
- Share your own constraints, set up personal ones?
