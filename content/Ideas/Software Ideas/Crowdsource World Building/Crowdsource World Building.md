# Overview

- Many people come together to write stories that flesh out a world
- The stories take place in different places and/or different times
- More interactive?
    - Online platform
    - Interactive map you click on different areas and get stories from there
- Instead of just hearing about a war 100s of years ago you can read a story taking place then
- Github style sharing
    - One main source of the writing that everyone can take from
    - People edit it the source on their own computer online/offline
    - Then submit their changes which can then be approved or not
- Campfire layout for stories within project
    - Each story has a directory for organization (maps, character, relationships, etc.)
    - Overall layout
- What to work on/possible problems
    - Ownership
    - Quality control
    - Getting people willing to write about a world that's not their own
    - Continuity in the world
