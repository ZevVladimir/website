- En 1587 Reccared se convirtió al catolicismo influenciado por San Leander
- Hubo una rebelión de los asriannos liderara por el obispo Athaloc que ganó Reccared y algunas otras pero los católicos ganaron todos
- Para los judíos

With regards to the Jews, Reccared did not initiate  
any new legislation that would have targeted them, though  
there were five ecclesiastical councils held under his  
authority. Further, Reccared abolished the death penalty for  
Jews who proselytized.
