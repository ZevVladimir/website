## Ingredients
- [ ] 6 lb honey
- [ ] 2 liters pomegranate juice 
- [ ] Water to top off
- [ ] 2 teaspoon Yeast nutrient
    - Split up
- [ ] 5g packet of ICV-D47

## Instructions
- **Sanitize equipment**
- Bloom the yeast in warm water
- Add honey, pomegranate juice, and water to 2 gallon bucket
- Take a gravity reading of the liquid
    - Initial measurement **28 brix**
- Sprinkle yeast into fermenter and add 1/2tsp yeast nutrient
- Cover with sanitized cover and air lock.
- Day 0: 2025/12/27
- **Day 1, 3, 5, 7, 8:** Degas mead by gently stirring, twirling the carboy carefully, or using a wine de-gasser
- **Day 2, 4, 6:** Gently stir and add 4.5g Fermaid-K and 2g of DAP (here just used ~1/2tsp each time nutrient)
- Once fermentation has mostly stopped move to secondary
- Test and if needed add acid blend for more acid flavor
- At 04/02/2026 racked from bucket to two 1 gallon car boys brix was ~14
- Using sanitized equipment, rack the mead off the sediment, into a clean, freshly sanitized carboy. Cap with sanitized airlock, leave it alone for a few months
- If mead remains hazy add pectic enzyme
- When there is NO more fermenting activity for a month or so (no bubbles in the airlock, no more sediment being produced, you can move on to bottling.
- If stabilizing, follow the instructions on your selected type of wine stabilizer to stop fermentation. For potassium sorbate, this needs to be done 2-3 days before bottling.
- Then bulk age depending on taste?
- Using sanitized equipment, take a gravity reading, then rack the mead into clean, sanitized bottles. Cork.
