A well written book that covers an interesting alien encounter where the aliens have saved the humans from nuclear war that has devastated Earth. We then follow Lilith who is chosen by the aliens to lead a group of humans back to Earth hundreds of years later. However, the aliens aren't doing this for free, a point made clear by their emphasis on "trade". Specifically trading things that humans are good at for what the aliens are good at as well. This is done literally with the adjustment of genes and a mixing of biological things that make each species unique. Touches on ideas of what makes humans human and an interesting alien arrival story. Written well it is short and easy to read through.
