An interesting book about humans from another planet being essentially enslaved by an alien race that is focused on expanding and integrating the aliens it comes across into its empire if they are useful and doing away with them if they're not. We focus mostly on the humans jumping back and forth between perspectives and this helps build up who every person is and their unique aspects. I think it was a little basic at times but as a first book in the series I think that makes sense and it will continuously be built up. I also liked the alienness of the world in which they're transported but it is kept manageable by them being trapped in a couple of rooms for most of the book. Dafyd is also an interesting character who I'm excited to learn more about. The swarm was also a little weird and again I'm assuming will lead to a much larger reveal later on. So very interesting start and premise but will need to see the next books to see if I really like it
