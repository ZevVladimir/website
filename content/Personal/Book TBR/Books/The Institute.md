I like Stephen King's writing a lot, the first chapter especially for some reason really drew me in (I think it just hit my current desire to just go out and travel and discover things more than anything). Other than that the book is solid but nothing particularly special, kids are kidnapped that have slight telekinesis or telepathy and are taken to the institute where they are effectively tortured/experimented on in order to protect the world by assassinating people that would lead to nuclear war. The characters are solid but again nothing super special and after a certain point everything feels expected. The escape sequence was pretty well done though. But I do feel like with these books that the magical powers kind of cheapen it especially since everyone is super quick to move on once they see it. But also to be fair to it, I enjoyed reading and there were parts where I just blazed through it and didn't put it down.
