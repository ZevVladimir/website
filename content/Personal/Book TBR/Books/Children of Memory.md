An interesting book that I did somewhat struggle to get through, mainly because I don't think I felt a strong connection to the characters or the plot especially once it became very clear it was some sort of time loop/time warping. But by the end I think I could appreciate what the book was trying to explore and I think that's what sci-fi is really good for and I would like to approach with any writing I do. The exploration of what life really is and what that means for how we interact with it is a really interesting and important question that often arises in sci-fi but I haven't seen explored in so many ways until this book. I'm not sure how much was struggling to write engaging alien characters versus just an impossibility to both convey convincing and actually unique aliens and to make them interesting to us as readers. But I think it's cool to see it experimented with and would like to read more both in this series and other sci-fi that approaches this topic
