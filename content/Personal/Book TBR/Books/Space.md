Interesting book, basically a retelling of of America's space program in the 1900s with an emphasis on the people involved. It is a bit long and takes a while to get anywhere to be honest, and while this is good for character development by letting us see their growth over the decades it didn't feel like it was done well enough to justify that. I think this was particularly hurt by the dialogue which was, when present, written like a script for a play. But it was still enjoyable oncee they got to the space part and recognized some stuff from [[Challenger]].
