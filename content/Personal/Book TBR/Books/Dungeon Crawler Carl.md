While its definitely not the best written book, generally felt pretty predictable and is a genre that has been done before many times I still really enjoyed it once it got going. It has a good combination of an interesting story and setting and likable enough characters (although the attempts to add depth to them definitely fall short) that it is just fun to read and I was able to blaze through it. Although to give credit there is mention throughout about just how fucked up all of this is which I think is good and can get glossed over in other similar books. I think its a combination of that fantasy of what if I was put in a video game actually coming true, the enjoyment of cool video game mechanics, and then all the weird creatures and people. I think it also gave me the sense that they were trying to build up for a lot more complexity in the future books that I'm excited to see if it can come to fruition.
