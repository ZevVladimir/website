A very long, and in my opinion unnecessarily long book. It reads like an American novel (which it is so not real surprise) and has a very descriptive language that goes beyond making the setting feel alive and into I can skim the next few pages without missing much that was important. We follow someone who is besides the person of most power and influence which is interesting and similar to something like great gatsby where you see a person of power through the pov of someone who is near them. But I couldn't bring myself to care for him or really anyone in the story. I think I would probably enjoy this more if I could get through this in less than three weeks but maybe thats just part of the problem.
