It took me a little bit to really get into the book but once it got going I was pretty immersed in what was happening. I don't know too much about the 1968 protests but it was an interesting backdrop to a novel that was more about people and how events in their pasts shape them and how that gets passed on generationally to others. I found the video game aspect of the book a little bit odd? For what it ended up being it felt like a bit too heavily emphasized, but maybe I'm just being too defensive of video games. It also had some interesting comments on protest and whether it actually means anything/worth doing. Especially since we see that the main characters mom was there for a month and then that proceeded to completely shape (and in some senses ruin) a large part of the rest of her life. Where she felt forced to return to a life she didn't want.
