interesting book covering 4 years 1968-89 in NYC with an emphasis on the mayor, Ed Koch, and surrounding characters and overarching issues of racism, honelessness, the crack epidemic, and AIDS. Was not boring with regular searching between different characters dealing with different crises and issues. Was written very recently but even so definitely interesting to see how many of the current days issues (Trump included) were very much present back then and in some way much worse. Interested to read the other book I got on this time period and see how they compare.
