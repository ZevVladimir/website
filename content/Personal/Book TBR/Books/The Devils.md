A pretty classic Abercrombie book with the brutal, grimdark, humor throughout that still is good. Overall the story is interesting although somewhat predictable, but with enough twists and setups that it is still interesting and I'd probably want to read the next book. But it did feel a bit too long for what it was, where for most of the book it was variations on a theme. The group ends up fighting some big group of baddies then one of the group especially stands up and does something to win the day. The characters were pretty good, interesting to see a different take on some magical creatures while still keeping it mostly the same. I guess in summary it was well done but nothing special, although this might be because I read this after the other series he had written and already know his style.
