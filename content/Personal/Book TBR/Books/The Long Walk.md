Overall well done in typical king fashion where he manages to make anything gripping. Managed to read this in less than a day (mostly thanks to American fucking up my flight three times) and it felt short but sweet and didn't overstay it's welcome. It was able to develop the relationships between the characters relatively well for the short amount of time they were there. The critique of society still holds up pretty well today but does it in a way where you think about how absurd what the book is talking about is but then so much similar happens in the real world. Sometimes it did jump out that walking a 15 min mile is really fast lol I think the point could've been made even if it was a "leisurely" 20 minute mile because at a certain point it doesn't really matter and just becomes a mental battle
