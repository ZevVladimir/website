A decent book but I never felt myself fully engaged and brought into the world or particularly caring about any of the characters. I feel like this is often a problem that is difficult to avoid in sci-fi when following non-human characters and while it can be very interesting to see how they act it can make it hard to care about what they are actually doing. There was some interesting aspects with the multiple bodies and how that plays into overall actions of the one "person" and I think it could be interesting to see it explored further. I think a lot of it could have been helped with stronger side character building which was present but didn't just didn't do enough for me. Not sure if I'll go out of my way to read the next couple but maybe something else by the author if I come across it
