Again just a fun book to read that's entertaining and easy to just blaze through. It does start setting up more of a larger world with more complex ethics and character growth. At times it's a bit heavy handed in doing this but it never felt too bad. The world is also consistently unique enough to not just feel like a blatant copy of similar stories while also being familiar enough so someone is able to easily follow along. Definitely interested to see the whole story unfold and if they're able to keep the dungeon interesting and keep developing Carl and Donut and the other people they meet. Also wanna mention how trippy it is to see Zev written throughout. I also feel like I'm getting similar feelings to [[One Piece Vol 1-Present]] where there are overarching story arcs with their own specific villains and side characters and unique setting and then they move on after it's complete but most things do play into a larger more meaningful overarching story. It also has similar idea of building a party around a central protagonist each with specific powers and personalities (which as I'm writing is pretty general lol but definitely still one piece vibes)
