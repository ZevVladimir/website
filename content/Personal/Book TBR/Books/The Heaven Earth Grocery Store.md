Well written and overall a good read. A bit predictable at times but I think the focus was more on the characters and making each one feel alive and unique. It was also an interesting look at America from not that long ago. What especially stuck out was how initially the jews and the blacks stuck together and got close but as soon as the jews were able to integrate a little and make money they left (except Chona especially and Moshe to some extent). The idea of new immigrants constantly trying to fit in and adjust to the new way of life which to some extent they are able to do especially compared to the black people who continue to still be treated horribly throughout. I thought the Dodo storyline was interesting and unique to me. There was a random sidenote about modern technology that was thrown in there that really took me out of the book and a couple other more minor things that affected the flow but nothing that was too bad.
