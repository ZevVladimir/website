An interesting book that gives a serious take on how the colonization of Mars would look. It at times can go on for a while, particularly the last chapter is primarily just a description of the drive through many canyons of Mars. There is a stronger symbolism about how the original mars has been completely destroyed by humans and that is a consistent argument throughout. About the preservation of what once was against the inexorable drive of humans to terraform and extract resources. The characters were solid, some more interesting than others and I would like to have seen a little bit more for when everything started going to shit.
