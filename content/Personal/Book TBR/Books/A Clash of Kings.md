Overall enjoyable epic fantasy. The scope of the story is really cool and even though I know the broad strokes of what will happen I'm still interested throughout. The writing of women can be pretty atrocious and there are definitely some things that just feel like he had a fantasy and just wrote it in... I might continue reading the rest of the series since there are only so many epic fantasy books but I also don't know if I want to be super invested in a series that's pretty much never going to be finished.
