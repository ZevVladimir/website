Very interesting concept where we follow not only humans but spiders on a terraformed (by old humans) planet as they evolve through the help of a virus that affects their genetic information to encourage quick evolution. This is probably the best part of the book, a unique look at an alien race and how they progress from "wild" animals to integrating the humans after beating them. I was not super attached to the characters, both because the spiders died (although I liked the continuity of the names, made it easier to follow) and because the humans kept going in and out of stasis. But they were interesting as a way to view humanity through which I think was more of the intention. It felt like a distillation of humanity (which it objectively was, the last vestiges of humanity on a generation ship) and all the things that humans do wrong or right.
