An interesting book, that is engaging I think because of its large focus on sex, especially the first half of the book and the limited writing that moves quickly. The main character is not a person who says much, I don't think there was really a conversation where he didn't speak in 1-3 word fragments, and despite this and despite not finding himself with much purpose in life ends up living both a very full life and a very interesting one. He starts as being groomed and then raped by an older woman as a child which he never really comes to terms with or ever considers a deeper meaning behind. To serving in the army in Iraq, to leaving Hungary to England where he eventually has an affair with a wealthy woman who decides to marry him after her husband dies giving him a very rich life, to having both his son and then his wife die in a car crash and moving back to the same village in Hungary once again alone and with no strong purpose in life. I saw a lot of comments about how this is supposed to relate to being a man but as a man I can't say I really understood that but could maybe see that in what you hear about in other people's lives. I think the most impactful part is how it completely captures a person's life with the ups and downs and by not giving the main character a strong identifiable personality it can feel more general and potentially applicable to your own life. It was a striking book and one I read really quickly but it also left me with some weird feelings and I'm not really sure I would even recommend it unless someone was looking for an interesting reading experience.
