This recipe calls for an 8x4-inch loaf pan, which produces a tall loaf. The recipe will also work in an 8 1/2x4 1/2-inch loaf pan, producing a slightly shorter bread. Since the pan is a bit larger, the bake time is shorter: 47 to 57 minutes. A 9x5-inch pan will yield an especially short but still delicious bread; bake for 45 to 55 minutes.

# Ingredients

- 2 to 3 medium (7" to 7-7/8" long) [very ripe bananas](https://www.simplyrecipes.com/how-to-freeze-bananas-5197263), peeled (about 1 1/4 to 1 1/2 cups mashed)
- 1/3 cup (76g) butter, unsalted or salted, melted
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda (not baking powder)
- 1 pinch salt
- 1/2 cup brown sugar
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 1/2 cups (205g) all-purpose flour
- ~1/2 cup chocolate chips

# Method

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. Butter an 8x4-inch loaf pan.
3. In a mixing bowl, mash the ripe bananas with a fork until completely smooth.
4. Stir the melted butter into the mashed bananas.
5. Mix in the baking soda and salt.
6. Stir in the sugar, beaten egg, and vanilla extract.
7. Mix in the flour
8. Pour the batter into your prepared loaf pan
9. Bake for 55 to 65 minutes at 350°F (175°C), or until a toothpick or wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean. A few dry crumbs are okay; streaks of wet batter are not. If the outside of the loaf is browned but the center is still wet, loosely tent the loaf with foil and continue baking until the loaf is fully baked.
