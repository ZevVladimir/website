# Ingredients

- 1 cup oatmilk
- 1 tbsp coconut cream
- 5-6 tbsp [[Chai Inspired Syrup]]
- 1 tsp [[Chai Inspired Spice Mix]]
- 1 tsp alcohol (used bourbon last time)
- 1/4 tsp salt

  

Combine all ingredients and mix and then freeze for ~24 hours before using Ninja
