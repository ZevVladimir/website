### Ingredients

- 1/4 cup dijon mustard
- 1/4 cup stone ground mustard
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 sprigs rosemary, chopped
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp pepper
- 1 1/2 pounds chicken tenderloins (about 9-10 pieces)
- 1 tbsp olive oil

### Instructions

- Combine both mustards, honey, lemon juice, rosemary, salt and pepper in a large bowl. Add chicken tenderloins to bowl and toss to combine. Place into the refrigerator to marinate for at least 30 minutes or overnight.
- Heat olive oil in large pan over medium heat. Once hot, add chicken to the pan in a single layer, reserve the marinade. Depending on the size of your pan, you may need to cook chicken in 2 separate batches and clean pan in between. Cook chicken for 3 minutes. Once it's golden, flip the chicken and add the marinade into the pan so the sauce can heat up. Cook for 3 more minutes, or until chicken is cooked through. The sauce should become hot and bubbly.
- Transfer to a serving dish and let cool 5 minutes before serving.
