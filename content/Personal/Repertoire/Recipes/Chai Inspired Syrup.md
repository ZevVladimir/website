- 250 ml water
- 100 g brown sugar
- 100 g white sugar
- 1 [vanilla bean](https://amzn.to/32F1rtx), sliced in half lengthwise (or 2 teaspoons [vanilla bean](https://amzn.to/32F1rtx) paste or 2 tsp vanilla extract)
- 2 teaspoons [[Chai Inspired Spice Mix]]

---

**Instructions**

1. Combine the water, brown sugar, white sugar and vanilla bean in a saucepan. Bring to a boil and then reduce to a simmer for 5 minutes, stirring often.
2. Add the chai spice mix and continue simmering for another 8 minutes.
3. Remove the vanilla bean pods and strain the syrup through a fine mesh strainer.
4. Fill into clean glass bottles, let them cool completely and then store in the fridge.
