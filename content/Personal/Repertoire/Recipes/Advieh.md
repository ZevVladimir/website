# Ingredients
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1 teaspoon ground rose petals
- 1 teaspoon ground cardamom
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin

Mix together and keep in an airtight container.
