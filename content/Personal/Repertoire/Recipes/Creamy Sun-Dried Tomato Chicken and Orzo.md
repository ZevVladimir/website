# Ingredients

- [ ] 2 tablespoons [extra virgin olive oil](https://www.target.com/p/organic-extra-virgin-olive-oil-16-9-fl-oz-good-38-gather-8482/-/A-82102661#lnk=sametab)
- [ ] 1 pound [boneless, skinless chicken breasts or small thighs](https://www.target.com/p/organic-boneless-38-skinless-chicken-breasts-frozen-2lbs-good-38-gather-8482/-/A-15330917#lnk=sametab)
- [ ] 1 teaspoon [dried oregano](https://www.target.com/p/organic-oregano-0-8oz-good-38-gather-8482/-/A-77616564#lnk=sametab)
- [ ] 1 teaspoon [paprika](https://www.target.com/p/ground-paprika-2-12oz-good-38-gather-8482/-/A-77587875#lnk=sametab)
- [ ] 1/4-1/2 teaspoon [crushed red pepper flakes](http://l.thrv.me/HBH4991-simply-organic-crushed-red-peppe)
- [ ] [kosher salt and black pepper](https://www.target.com/p/morton-coarse-kosher-salt-16oz/-/A-13170448#lnk=sametab)
- [ ] 2 tablespoons [butter](https://www.target.com/p/organic-salted-butter-1lb-good-gather-8482/-/A-54575962#lnk=sametab)
- [ ] 1 [medium shallot, chopped](https://www.target.com/p/shallots-price-per-lb/-/A-14993271#lnk=sametab)
- [ ] 2 cloves [garlic, minced or grated](https://www.target.com/p/spice-world-fresh-whole-garlic-3ct-bag/-/A-14917318#lnk=sametab)
- [ ] 1 cup [dry orzo pasta](https://www.target.com/p/signature-orzo-16oz-good-38-gather-8482/-/A-79283464#lnk=sametab)
- [ ] 1/3 cup dry white wine, such as Pinot Grigio or Sauvignon Blanc
- [ ] 1 cup [heavy cream](https://www.target.com/p/heavy-whipping-cream-1pt-good-38-gather-8482/-/A-54550905#lnk=sametab) [[Vegan Heavy Cream]]
- [ ] 2 teaspoons [Dijon mustard](https://www.target.com/p/maille-dijon-original-mustard-8-9oz/-/A-84887036#lnk=sametab)
- [ ] 1/3 cup [grated parmesan cheese](https://www.target.com/p/grated-parmesan-cheese-cup-5oz-good-38-gather-8482/-/A-54589409#lnk=sametab)
- [ ] 2 cups [fresh baby spinach](https://www.target.com/p/baby-spinach-10oz-good-38-gather-8482/-/A-54555552#lnk=sametab)
- [ ] 1/2 cup [oil packed sun-dried tomatoes, oil drained](https://www.target.com/p/alessi-sun-dried-tomatoes-in-olive-oil-7oz/-/A-52487155#lnk=sametab)
- [ ] [juice of 1 lemon](https://www.target.com/p/lemon-each/-/A-15013629#lnk=sametab)
- [ ] fresh rosemary, for serving (optional)

# Instructions

- 1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees F.
- 2. Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet set over medium-high heat. Rub the chicken with 1 tablespoon olive oil, the oregano, paprika, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper. When the oil is shimmering, add the chicken. Sear on both sides until golden, about 3-5 minutes per side. Remove the chicken from the skillet.
- 3. To the same skillet, add the butter and shallot, cooking until fragrant, about 3 minutes. Add the garlic and orzo, cooking until lightly golden, 2-3 minutes.
- Add the wine and de-glaze the pan. Add 1 1/2 cups water.
- Bring to a boil, cook 3-5 minutes, then add the cream, mustard, parmesan, spinach, and sun-dried tomatoes, stirring until the spinach has wilted.
- Slide the chicken and any juices left on the plate back into the skillet. Transfer to the oven and cook, uncovered for 10-15 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through.
- 4. Serve the chicken topped with lemon juice and rosemary, if desired.
