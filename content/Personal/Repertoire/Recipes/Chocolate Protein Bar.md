**Yields:** 12-16 bars (depending on how you cut them) **Prep time:** 15 minutes **Cook time (oats):** 20-30 minutes **Chill time:** 30 minutes

**Ingredients:**

- 1/2 cup (120g) cooked steel-cut oats (see instructions below for cooking)
	- Tried 1/4 cup rolled oats 60 g after cooking and kinda dried out...
- 1 to 1.5 cups (120g-180g) chocolate pea protein powder (182g last time)
- 1/2 cup (128g) [[Sunflower Butter]] (165g last time)
- 2 tablespoon chia seeds
- 1 teaspoon vanilla bean paste
- Pinch of salt
- 1/4 cup (45g) chocolate chips 
- 2-4 tablespoons oat milk (as needed for consistency)
- Chocolate chips
- Flaky salt (if desired)
- If making caramel: [[Dairy-free Caramel]]
- If chocolate robing more chocolate chips melted

**Instructions:**

1. **Prepare the steel-cut oats:**
    - Bring 1/2 cup of dry steel-cut oats and 2 cup of water to a boil in a small saucepan.
    - Reduce the heat to a simmer and cook for 20-30 minutes, or until the oats are tender and have absorbed most of the liquid.
    - Let the cooked oats cool **completely** to room temperature.
 2. If using rolled oats
	 - Use 1/4 cup rolled oats
	 - Use 1/2 cup water/milk 
	 - Then simmer for 5-7 minutes
	 - Or can just microwave for ~2 min
- Prepare [[Dairy-free Caramel]]
1. In a large bowl, mix together the sunflower butter, vanilla bean paste, and salt until smooth.
2. Add the chocolate pea protein powder, chia seeds, and the cooled, cooked steel-cut oats to the wet mixture.
3. Start by adding just 2 tablespoons of oat milk.
4. Mix with hands until a thick, uniform dough forms. If the dough is too dry to hold together, add the remaining oat milk one tablespoon at a time until the desired consistency is reached.
5. **Fold in chocolate chips:**
6. Transfer the dough to the pan and press it down firmly and evenly using a spatula or your hands
7. Place pan in fridge for ~30 min
8. Then slice into bars
9. Add caramel once cooled in a layer across all the bars
10. Place the pan in the freezer for at least 1 hour to allow the bars to firm up.
11. Melt chocolate
12. Cut up the sheet into bars and dip each on in the chocolate making sure to fully encase the caramel
13. Sprinkle flakey salt before the chocolate cools if desired
14. Place back in the refrigerator and cool
