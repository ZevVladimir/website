- 2 pounds lean ground beef/chicken
- 1 onion diced
- 1 jalapeño seeded and finely diced
- Chilis in adobo
- 4 cloves garlic minced
- 2 ½ tablespoons chili powder (and more)
- Smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon cumin (and more)
- 1 green bell pepper seeded and diced
- 14.5 ounces crushed tomatoes 1 can (I just used one large can of whole tomatoes and smashed them while cooking)
-  14.5 ounces canned diced tomatoes with juices
- 19 ounces canned red kidney beans drained and rinsed
- 1 ½ cups beef/vegetable broth
- 1 cup beer
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- Dash of cinnamon
- Dash of cocoa powder
- Dash of vinegar
- salt and black pepper to taste

- Combine ground beef and 1 ½ tablespoons chili powder. 
- In a large pot, brown ground beef, onion, jalapeno, garlic and spices. Drain any fat.
- Add tomato paste and let carmelize
- Add in remaining ingredients and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer uncovered 45-60 minutes or until chili has reached desired thickness.
