**Beef and Broccoli** (serve with white rice)

High for 3 hours, feeds 1-2

- 1 lb. boneless chuck roast, sliced into strips
- 1/4 cup low sodium soy sauce
- 2 Tbs. brown sugar
- 1 cup of broccoli florets
- 1/2 Tbs. sesame oil
- 2 cloves of garlic, minced
- (pepper to taste)

i cut my chuck roast into small chunks, just because i liked it better that way. i also ended up using bragg's liquid aminos in place of the soy sauce (always make sure what you have...), but it still turned out great
