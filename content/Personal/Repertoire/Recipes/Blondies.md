# Ingredients
- 1/2 cup butter (113-115g)
- 2 scant cups brown sugar (~300g)
- 2 eggs
- 2 cups flour (250g)
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 cup chocolate chips
- 8x8 pan

# Steps
1. Preheat oven to $350\degree\mathrm{F}$
2. Melt butter
3. Mix in sugar
4. Mix in eggs
5. Mix in everything else. Don't stir too much
6. Oil pan
7. Bake in oven for 25-30 minutes. Should look set in the center and a knife should come out clean.
